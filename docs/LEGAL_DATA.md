# Source provenance and legal-data licensing

The bundled `examples/demo-manifest.json` is **fictional** and has no legal authority. No actual legal database is shipped in the public starter package.

The earlier 1,335-article Iranian Civil Code document assembled during product research was taken from the third-party project `GeekNeuron/IranLegalHUB` (MIT repository license):
https://github.com/GeekNeuron/IranLegalHUB/tree/main/laws/civil_code

That material is not an independently verified official consolidation, and source repository licensing alone does not demonstrate that all rights, original-source authenticity, later amendments and effective dates have been verified.

The optional converter `tools/convert_iranlegalhub_civil.py` can turn locally supplied source volumes into a manifest. It labels the dataset unverified. This manifest should be stored in the private research corpus until each article is checked against authoritative legislation.

For redistributing any upstream software or substantial associated material, preserve its notices and attribution. Keep official provenance and evidence per document. Confirm publication rights and obligations with a qualified adviser prior to commercial rollout.
